The Prosperity Project is the umbrella for everything I'm thinking, reading, and
building around progress studies — the question of how human flourishing has
actually compounded over time, what conditions made that possible, and what we
might do (technically, economically, institutionally) to make more of it happen.

It cuts across:

- **Notes** — what I'm reading on growth, energy, state capacity, science
  funding, and the history of progress.
- **Writing** — where I work out my own beliefs on which interventions actually
  matter and why.
- **Projects** — anything I build that's downstream of those beliefs.

Anything tagged `prosperity-project` belongs to this theme.
